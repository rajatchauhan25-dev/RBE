General Instructions
  1. This is a full mock in the Mains pattern with sections in a fixed order: Maths, Reasoning, English, General Awareness. Every section has its own timer (shown top right) and starts only when you begin that section.
  2. You can move freely between the questions of the current section only. To go to the next section you must press Submit Section, or wait until the section timer ends — then the section is submitted automatically. A submitted section cannot be opened again, and you cannot jump ahead to a later section.
  3. General Awareness is the last section, so it has the Submit Test button.
  4. The question palette on the right shows the status of each question using the symbols explained on the next page.
  5. Click on the number of a question in the palette to go to it directly. Your answer to the question you are leaving is not saved unless you press Save & Next or Mark for Review first.
  6. To select an answer, click the radio button next to the option. To deselect it, click the same option again or press Clear Response.
  7. To save your answer you MUST click Save & Next. To flag a question and come back later, click Mark for Review. A question that is answered and marked for review is still evaluated.
  8. Each correct answer carries - mark(s) and each wrong answer deducts - mark(s). Unattempted questions carry no marks.
  9. You can Pause the test; your progress is saved in this browser and the running section timer waits for you.
  10. Do not refresh the page or press the Back button during the test.
The different symbols used in the next pages are shown below. Please go through them and understand their meaning before you start the test.
1You have not visited the question yet.
2You have not answered the question.
3You have answered the question.
4You have NOT answered the question, but have marked the question for review.
5You have answered the question, but marked it for review. This answer will be considered for evaluation.
